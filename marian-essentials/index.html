The founder

Thick, not see-through — the test every piece here has to pass.

Marian Essentials started as a stall in Lusaka New Market and grew on one promise: nothing leaves the shop that you would be embarrassed to wear twice a week. Weight, stitch and colour are checked by hand before anything is posted.

What began as a few rails is now a shop with 28.7K people watching what lands next — and parcels going out to every province in the country.
